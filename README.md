# floor-plan-to-3d

The prompt from my reel "Floor plan to real film" ([@aipractically](https://www.instagram.com/aipractically/)).

You give Claude a floor plan and one real measurement. It builds a 3D model of the apartment that you can walk through in the browser.

Copy the prompts from [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md).

## How the film in the reel was made

1. Claude built the 3D model from the plan, scaled with the width of a door.
2. I furnished the model and recorded a camera walk through it.
3. A video model (Kling) turned that walk into real-looking footage, so the layout of the film is the layout of the plan.

Steps 2 and 3 are my own pipeline and are not in this repo. The prompt in PROMPT.md covers step 1.
